# Manual or External PIX Contract

## GENERIC-PROMOTABLE

An external/manual PIX is an administrative representation of money received outside the integrated gateway. It is not a simulated gateway payment. Use it only when the product and current system explicitly support the workflow and a financial authority has authorized the exact subject and amount.

Required evidence and provenance:

- actual amount received in integer cents and currency;
- actual payment time when known, including source timezone semantics;
- original external reference only when it was genuinely provided;
- administrative actor and authorization;
- reason and evidence classification;
- explicit statement that the gateway was not involved;
- previous and intended financial state.

If no transaction reference exists, do not invent one. Record `transactionReferenceProvided=false`, a null/absent external reference, or the equivalent supported semantic. An internal correlation or idempotency key may be generated by the system, but it must be labeled as internal and must never be presented as a TXID, NSU, EndToEndId, provider payment ID, or other provider evidence.

## Distinct financial cases

- **Gateway-confirmed payment:** authenticated provider evidence and the gateway workflow.
- **External/manual payment:** authorized external evidence and an explicit administrative provider/method.
- **Courtesy:** product-granted participation without payment; never encode it as manual PIX of zero.
- **Discount:** pricing adjustment before payment; not confirmation.
- **Pending or expired payment:** no confirmed receipt under the required evidence path.
- **Cancelled/refunded:** use only a supported transition and compensating workflow; never improvise by rewriting history.

If courtesy, refund, or cancellation is not modeled, stop with `REVIEW REQUIRED: PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED`.

## FUNPACE-SPECIFIC

### Representation

- Use the current explicit administrative provider path, presently `manual_pix`, only for a real external PIX that did not involve InfinitePay.
- Preserve `infinitePayInvolved=false`, the real amount, the real `paid_at` when known, actor, reason, and evidence metadata. Do not populate `provider_payment_id`, checkout, or InfinitePay response fields without real provider evidence.
- The current implementation may use a `manual_pix_<registration>` value as an internal correlation/deduplication key in a gateway-shaped column. Treat it solely as a synthetic local key, never as an external transaction reference, and verify that downstream logic preserves that distinction.
- Use the current administrative payment event type and audit actions. Never emit `verified_paid`, an InfinitePay event, or an artificial webhook for this flow.

### Preconditions and atomicity

- Start read-only: identify the entity, inspect current and historical registrations, payment/event/audit state, partner/coupon, amount, lot, bib, and external evidence.
- Do not overwrite an active `pending_payment` or `paid` registration through a shortcut. Preserve expired/cancelled history and use the architecture's supported new-record path when that is the proven design.
- Validate the registration payload and keep marketing consent ungranted unless it was actually collected. Payment authorization is not marketing consent.
- Prove partner eligibility and pricing before confirmation. Compare original price, discount amount, final price, received amount, registration amount, and payment amount.
- Use the established transaction and locks for registration, payment, lot, bib, payment event, and audit. Stop on capacity, duplicate active registration, amount mismatch, bib/event collision, or ambiguous prior execution.

### Audit and verification

- Audit actor, authorization, reason, evidence class, received amount, paid time, method/provider, provider involvement, previous/next state, and applicable partner snapshot.
- Capture lot baseline and independently verify the post-state by new registration/payment identity; do not attribute a global concurrent delta blindly.
- Verify both registration and payment paid state, amount consistency, the administrative event, audit entries, bib uniqueness, lot effect, and absence of fabricated provider identifiers.
- Email, Sheets, Meta/CAPI, dashboards, and summaries are non-financial side effects. Their failure does not justify replaying the manual payment operation.
- Never delete historical or audit records to reverse a mistake. Use an explicitly supported compensating/refund/cancellation process or stop for product and financial review.
